01 / ABOUT

An offensive mindset.
A defensive purpose.

Modern security demands more than assumptions. Meaningful assessments are grounded in observable behavior, technical evidence, and a clearly defined scope.

SALES CYBERSECURITY SOLUTIONS is a Brazil-based cybersecurity practice with a focus on offensive security. Our work centers on identifying and validating security weaknesses in applications, APIs, infrastructure, and controlled research environments to support informed risk reduction.

OUR FOCUS

Responsible research. Reproducible evidence. Clear communication of technical risk and remediation priorities.

OUR ENGAGEMENT PRINCIPLES

Authorization comes first.

Security testing is performed only in company-owned environments or against third-party systems with explicit prior authorization. Engagements must have defined boundaries, responsible handling of findings, and respect for system availability and data confidentiality.
